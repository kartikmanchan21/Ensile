Heliocentric Theory - Copernicus

Introduction to Copernicus' Heliocentric Theory

Nicholas Copernicus proposed the Heliocentric Theory in the 16th century, stating that the Earth and other planets revolve around the Sun, challenging the prevailing geocentric view that placed Earth at the center of the universe.

Key Points

  • The Sun is at the center of the universe.
  • The Earth and other planets revolve around the Sun.
  • This model laid the foundation for modern astronomy and the scientific revolution.
