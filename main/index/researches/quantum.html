Introduction to Quantum Mechanics

Quantum mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic levels. It introduces the concepts of wave-particle duality, quantization, and uncertainty.

Key Concepts

  • Wave-Particle Duality: Particles like electrons can exhibit both wave-like and particle-like properties.
  • Quantum Superposition: A quantum system can exist in multiple states at once until measured.
  • Uncertainty Principle: Proposed by Heisenberg, it states that certain pairs of physical properties cannot be simultaneously known with arbitrary precision.

Impact on Science

Quantum mechanics has led to the development of technologies such as semiconductors, lasers, and quantum computers. It also has philosophical implications, challenging our understanding of reality and determinism.
