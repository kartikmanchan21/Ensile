Big Bang Theory

Introduction to the Big Bang Theory

The Big Bang Theory is the prevailing cosmological model for the origin of the universe. It suggests that the universe began as a singularity about 13.8 billion years ago and has been expanding ever since.

Key Points

  • The universe began as a hot, dense point and expanded rapidly in a process called inflation.
  • The expansion of the universe is still ongoing, as evidenced by the redshift of distant galaxies.
  • The cosmic microwave background radiation is considered the "afterglow" of the Big Bang.

Impact on Science

The Big Bang Theory has revolutionized our understanding of the universe's origin and evolution. It has led to important discoveries such as the expansion of the universe and the understanding of dark energy.
