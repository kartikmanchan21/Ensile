Black Holes Theory

Introduction to Black Holes

Black holes are regions in spacetime where gravity is so intense that not even light can escape. They are formed when massive stars collapse under their own gravity, creating a singularity surrounded by an event horizon.

Key Features

  • Singularity: A point of infinite density at the center of a black hole.
  • Event Horizon: The boundary beyond which nothing can escape the black hole's gravitational pull.
  • Hawking Radiation: A theoretical process where black holes can emit radiation and slowly lose mass over time.

Impact on Science

Black holes have provided profound insights into the nature of gravity, spacetime, and quantum mechanics. They remain one of the most fascinating phenomena in modern physics.
