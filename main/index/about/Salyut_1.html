Philae - First Landing on a Comet

Background

On November 12, 2014, Philae became the first spacecraft to land on a comet as part of the European Space Agency's Rosetta mission.

Key Achievements

Philae's lander sent back data and images from the surface of Comet 67P, providing unprecedented insights into the composition of comets.

Scientific Contributions

Philae helped scientists study the basic building blocks of life by analyzing the chemical makeup of the comet's surface.

Summary

The Philae mission was a groundbreaking achievement in space exploration, expanding our understanding of the early solar system and the role comets play in the origins of life.
