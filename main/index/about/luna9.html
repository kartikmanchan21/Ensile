Luna 9 - First Soft Landing on the Moon

Background

Launched by the Soviet Union on January 31, 1966, Luna 9 became the first spacecraft to achieve a successful soft landing on the Moon.

Key Achievements

Luna 9 transmitted the first photographs from the lunar surface, which were received on Earth.

The spacecraft's success proved the feasibility of landing on the Moon and paved the way for subsequent missions.

Summary

Luna 9 marked a significant milestone in space exploration by proving that soft landings on other celestial bodies were possible, directly influencing future lunar missions.
