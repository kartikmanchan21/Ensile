Project Horizon - U.S. Army Concept Mission

Background

Project Horizon was a U.S. Army concept developed in 1959, aiming to establish a military base on the moon for strategic purposes.

Objectives

The primary goal was to establish a permanent manned military base on the moon to provide advantages in the Cold War.

The project also included proposals for the construction of facilities on the lunar surface for surveillance and communications.

Challenges

The high cost of the mission, as well as technological and logistical limitations, ultimately led to the cancellation of the project.

Summary

Though it was never realized, Project Horizon marked an early attempt to extend military and strategic presence into space, influencing future space programs.
