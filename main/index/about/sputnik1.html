Sputnik 1 - First Artificial Satellite

Background

Launched by the Soviet Union on October 4, 1957, Sputnik 1 was the world's first artificial satellite, marking the beginning of the space age.

Key Achievements

Sputnik 1's successful launch initiated the space race between the U.S. and the Soviet Union.

It sent radio signals back to Earth, which were tracked by ground stations worldwide.

Scientific Impact

The satellite's orbit and radio emissions helped scientists gain a better understanding of Earth's atmosphere and ionosphere.

Summary

Sputnik 1's launch not only marked a major technological achievement but also served as the starting point for global space exploration efforts.
