Hubble Space Telescope

Background

Launched in 1990, the Hubble Space Telescope has revolutionized our understanding of the universe, capturing incredible images of distant galaxies, nebulae, and other celestial phenomena.

Key Discoveries

Hubble provided the most accurate measurement of the universe's expansion rate.

The telescope played a crucial role in understanding dark energy and the rate at which the universe is expanding.

Scientific Contributions

Hubble's observations have improved our knowledge of black holes, star formation, and the nature of galaxies.

Summary

The Hubble Space Telescope remains one of the most important scientific instruments ever built, continuing to provide valuable data to scientists today.
