India's Space Exploration Timeline

1969: Formation of ISRO

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) was founded, marking the beginning of India’s space journey.

1975: Aryabhata - India's First Satellite

India launched its first satellite, Aryabhata, into space, marking a significant milestone in its space history.

2008: Chandrayaan-1 - First Moon Mission

Chandrayaan-1 was launched, marking India’s first successful mission to the Moon and discovering water molecules on the lunar surface.

2013: Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan)

India successfully reached Mars on its first attempt with the Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan), becoming the first country to do so.

2017: Record for Most Satellites Launched in a Single Mission

ISRO set a world record by launching 104 satellites in a single mission (PSLV-C37), showcasing its leadership in satellite launching.

2019: Chandrayaan-2 - Moon Landing Attempt

Chandrayaan-2, aimed at exploring the lunar south pole, launched, though the Vikram lander unfortunately lost contact with the orbiter.

2024: Gaganyaan - India’s First Manned Space Mission

India plans to send humans into space for the first time with the Gaganyaan mission, making significant strides in human space exploration.
